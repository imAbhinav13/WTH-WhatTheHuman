## Science perspective

Consciousness is related to the self and experienced reality through multi-sensory integration and self-modeling processes.

- The temporo-parietal junction (TPJ) is a key area for multi-sensory integration of bodily self-consciousness. [C6]
- The phenomenal self-model (PSM) theory views selves as processes, not objects, and a conscious self is a result of the system's identification with its self-model. [C7]
- Perceptual illusion of body swapping can be induced by a continuous match between visual and somatosensory information about the state of the body. [C8]

## Advaita Vedanta perspective

In Advaita Vedanta, consciousness is eternal, non-dual, and partless, witnessing intellect and the rest, and is the idea of 'I', a union of being and bliss, which is the higher Self.

- Consciousness is eternal, non-dual, partless, uniform, and witness of intellect and the rest. [C2]
- The Self never changes and is the real nature of things, to be known by the wise to the end of freedom from bondage. [C2]
- All knowledge and sense perception, in every state of consciousness, is possible only because the Self exists. [C1]

**Limitations:**

## Samkhya perspective

In Samkhya, consciousness is related to the self and experienced reality through the concept of pure subject-object-less consciousness, which is the inmost essence of a person, beyond any change.

- The self is a constant and unchangeable essence in man, beyond the limits of any change, described as pure subject-object-less consciousness, reality, and bliss. [C4]
- Consciousness stands in need of the aid of Nature in the completion of the production of its effect, Self-consciousness. [C5]
- The self, the pure cit, is neither illusory nor an abstraction; it is concrete but transcendent, giving unity to all the movements of the knowledge-composites of subtle stuff. [C3]

**Limitations:**
- The supplied evidence only provides a limited understanding of the relationship between consciousness and the self in Samkhya.
